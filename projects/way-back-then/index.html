Depicting my earliest memory, this painting shows little me sitting in a stroller facing a hospital wall while my mother gave birth to my younger brother. Painted in a surrealist style with muted, grounded tones, the piece tries to capture how early memories exist at the edge of consciousness—both distant and oddly vivid, a collision of clarity and reconstructive distortion. The muted palette was intentional; I can't truly remember what I felt in that moment, whether it was fear, curiosity, or simply the unknowingness of being a child.
