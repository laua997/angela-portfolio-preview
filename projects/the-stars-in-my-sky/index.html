This painting is based on a Christmas scene in London, where strings of decorative lights line the streets above a moving crowd. Rather than real stars, the lights are seasonal and manufactured, yet they still function the same way: offering direction and a fixed point to look toward when a space feels overwhelming. The painting became a way of thinking about how something small and temporary can still provide a sense of orientation.
