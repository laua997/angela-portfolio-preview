project overview

Year
2022
Medium
Charcoal on paper
Size
24x18in
Category
2D

A charcoal still life of a few items I used daily at the time, rendered with equal attention to reflect how personally significant each one was. The variety of textures—smooth surfaces, transparent plastic, creased fabric—was a deliberate challenge. The purple hair clip, always with me at school, and the cat photo holder, holding polaroids of friends, make this a snapshot of a specific moment in my past.
