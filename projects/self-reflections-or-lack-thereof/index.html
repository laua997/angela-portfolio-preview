project overview

Year
2024
Medium
Charcoal on paper
Size
51.5x37in
Category
2D

This piece explores the idea that we can only truly see ourselves when alone. The figure gazes into a mirror, yet her reflection remains hidden from the viewer, inviting contemplation on self-perception and the complexity of identity. As an overthinker, solitude carries a duality: it can feel like comfort or anxiety, and that tension is central to the work. White chalk highlights and the soft glow of a single lamp add texture and warmth, while the layered composition—bed in the foreground, figure in the middle, wardrobe behind—creates depth that mirrors the multiple dimensions of self-exploration.
